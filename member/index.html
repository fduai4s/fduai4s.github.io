Hao Zhang is an Associate Professor and doctoral supervisor at Fudan University, and a National Outstanding Mentor for Innovation and Entrepreneurship. Dr. Zhang studied physics at Nanjing University (B.S. and M.S., 1996–2003) and received a Ph.D. from the School of Information Science and Technology, Fudan University (2003–2006). From 2006 to 2025 Dr. Zhang was a faculty member of the Department of Optical Science and Engineering in the School of Information Science and Technology, and has been with the College of Future Information Technology at Fudan since 2025.

Dr. Zhang was a visiting scholar at the University of Konstanz, Germany (Sep 2009 – Aug 2010), the U.S. DOE Ames Laboratory (Oct 2016 – Sep 2017), and the School of Engineering, Rutgers University (Dec 2024 – Jun 2025).

Dr. Zhang has published more than 140 SCI papers, including 5 ESI highly cited papers and papers in high-impact journals such as Nature Communications, Advanced Materials and Light: Science & Applications. More than 40 papers appeared in journals with an impact factor of 10 or above; the work has been cited more than 6,100 times by others, more than 14 papers have each been cited over 100 times, and the h-index exceeds 42. Dr. Zhang serves as a reviewer for Chemical Reviews, Advanced Functional Materials, Physical Review Letters and other journals.

Research interests include AI applications in the semiconductor industry and photonics, high-throughput computation and novel properties of quantum and energy materials, and non-Hermitian systems and their realizations.
