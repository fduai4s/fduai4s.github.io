Hao Zhang · Fudan University

Welcome to the homepage of Hao Zhang's group at Fudan University. We bring artificial intelligence, first-principles computation and optics together to understand and design materials and devices.

Our research spans AI for science in materials and in the semiconductor industry, the computation and prediction of novel quantum materials, and non-Hermitian theory with applications in ultrafast optical systems. Results appear in journals such as Nature Communications, Advanced Materials and Light: Science & Applications; see our publications.
