Research

Our work spans four connected directions, from AI-driven materials discovery to the physics of quantum and non-Hermitian systems.

AI for Science in Materials AI for Semiconductors Computations and Predictions of Novel Quantum Materials Non-Hermitian Theory and Ultrafast Non-Hermitian Systems

AI for Science in Materials

We build machine-learning models and high-throughput workflows that predict crystal properties, generate new crystal structures and screen materials for energy applications.

  • AI models and high-throughput algorithms
  • Transformer architectures and large language models for materials research
  • Crystal structure generation algorithms
  • Materials databases for thermoelectrics and perovskite photovoltaics
  • Materials agents

AI for Semiconductors

We develop simulation software for the semiconductor industry, from first-principles codes to multiphysics device models and process simulation, accelerated with AI.

  • FiASP, a density functional theory code
  • Opto-thermo-electric multiphysics simulation software for semiconductor micro- and nano-devices
  • Simulation software for ferroelectric devices
  • Full-process simulation software for atomic layer deposition (ALD)
  • Virtual metrology and FPGA deployment of models

Computations and Predictions of Novel Quantum Materials

Using first-principles methods, we study excitons, electron–phonon coupling, topology and transport in two-dimensional and quantum materials.

  • Material properties, excitonic effects and electron–phonon coupling in 2D materials
  • Prediction and transport properties of topological materials
  • Quasiparticle behavior at surfaces and interfaces
  • First-principles studies of thermal transport, electronic transport and thermoelectric properties
  • Exciton condensation and exciton dynamics
  • Development of pseudopotential plane-wave density functional software
  • Electronic excited states and photo-induced phase transitions

Non-Hermitian Theory and Ultrafast Non-Hermitian Systems

We develop non-Hermitian theory and apply it to design and simulate optical parametric systems, topological circuits and ultrafast laser dynamics.

  • Non-Hermitian systems and their optical and circuit realizations
  • Non-Hermitian theory of absorptive optical parametric amplification
  • Design and simulation of topological circuits
  • Optical non-Hermitian theory and simulation of ultrafast laser dynamics
